## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the rules for accepting returns from customers of Northstar Trading Co. ("Northstar"), issuing refunds and credits, and restocking returned goods. It aims to make the process fair, consistent, and fast for customers while protecting Northstar from avoidable losses.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all wholesale customers purchasing goods from Northstar, and to all staff in Sales Operations, Customer Service, Warehouse Operations, and Finance who handle returns or refunds.

## 3. Definitions

- **Return Window:** The period during which a customer may request a return, starting from the delivery date shown on the proof of delivery.
- **Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA):** A reference number issued before any goods are sent back. Goods returned without an RMA are not accepted.
- **Standard Return:** A return of undamaged, unopened, or resaleable goods where the customer changed their mind or ordered in error.
- **Defective Return:** A return where goods are damaged, faulty, expired, or do not match the order.
- **Restocking Fee:** A deduction from the refund for Standard Returns.
- **Credit Note:** A credit issued against the customer's account instead of a cash refund.

## 4. Rules

**4.1 Return Window.** Standard Returns are accepted within 30 calendar days of delivery. Defective Returns are accepted within 60 calendar days of delivery.

**4.2 RMA Required.** Customer Service must issue an RMA before any return is accepted. The RMA is valid for 14 calendar days from issue.

**4.3 Restocking Fee.** A restocking fee of 15% of the invoice line value applies to Standard Returns. No restocking fee applies to Defective Returns or to returns caused by a Northstar error.

**4.4 Restocking Eligibility.** Goods may be restocked only if they are unopened, in original packaging, and pass a condition check by the Warehouse Supervisor within 3 business days of receipt. Goods that fail are quarantined and may not be resold.

**4.5 Refund Deadline.** Approved refunds must be paid within 7 business days of the condition check.

**4.6 Refund Method.** Refunds are paid to the original payment method. Where that is not possible, Northstar issues a Credit Note.

**4.7 Approval Thresholds.** Refunds and credits are approved as follows:

| Refund or Credit Value | Required Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to $1,000 | Customer Service Supervisor |
| $1,001 to $5,000 | Sales Operations Manager |
| Above $5,000 | Finance Controller |

**4.8 Repeat Return Review.** If a customer submits more than 3 Standard Returns in 90 days, the Sales Operations Manager must review the account before approving the next return.

**4.9 Maximum Refund Per Order.** Total refunds on a single invoice may not exceed the invoice value less any restocking fees.

## 5. Approval Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Customer Service Agent | Logs return requests and issues RMAs |
| Warehouse Supervisor | Performs condition checks |
| Customer Service Supervisor | Approves refunds up to $1,000 |
| Sales Operations Manager | Approves $1,001 to $5,000, owns this policy, reviews repeat returns |
| Finance Controller | Approves above $5,000 and processes payments |

## 6. Exceptions

- **Late Defective Returns:** A defective return received between 61 and 90 days after delivery may be accepted with Sales Operations Manager approval, if the defect was not detectable on delivery.
- **Key account terms:** Customers with a signed Key Account Agreement may have a different return window or restocking fee. Those terms override Sections 4.1 and 4.3 only where the agreement states them explicitly.
- **Stock recall:** During a supplier product recall, all returns of the recalled product are accepted with no restocking fee and no return window, subject to the Operations Director's written notice.

## 7. Review Procedure

The Sales Operations Manager reviews this policy every March with input from Finance and Warehouse Operations. Changes require CFO approval. Each version is published to the policy repository with its effective date. Previous versions are retained for 3 years.
